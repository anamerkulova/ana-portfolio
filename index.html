Branching Scenario: Maximizing Student Speaking Time

Category: E-Learning Module Stage: Testing prototype with teachers

The Problem

Language teachers struggle with student participation—in typical 90-minute lessons, students only speak for 10-15 minutes (11-17% of class time). They often avoid using AI to plan, or use it without clarifying the learning goal first.

Solution

I built a branching scenario to let teachers experience consequences of using AI at different stages of their planning.

Process

I followed an action-mapping approach, inspired by Cathy Moore's book. I started by defining the business goal—increase student speaking time from 15% to 50%—then worked backward to identify the specific actions teachers need to take. Through barrier analysis with real teachers, I identified that modeling activities was the highest-leverage action they weren't doing consistently.
