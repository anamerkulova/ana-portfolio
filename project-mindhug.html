Blended Learning Programme Design

Psychology + Cybersecurity CPD Course

Programme Design CPD Accredited Blended Learning 30 Learners

The Challenge

Design and deliver CPD-accredited blended learning programme for 30 coaches with no cybersecurity background, combining psychological principles with technical security concepts in accessible, engaging format.

My Approach

Collaborated with psychology and cybersecurity SMEs to translate complex technical content into relatable scenarios. Designed multimodal learning experience combining presentations, roleplay simulations, peer teaching activities, and performance-based assessment.

Applied instructional design principles (ADDIE, Bloom's Taxonomy) and adult learning theory to create scaffolded progression from foundational concepts to practical application.

Key Design Decisions

  • Translated technical jargon into relatable, real-world scenarios
  • Built scaffolded progression from awareness to application
  • Integrated roleplay simulations for skill practice
  • Designed peer teaching activities to reinforce learning
  • Created performance-based assessments aligned to learning objectives

Outcomes

95% Satisfaction
30 Coaches Trained
CPD Accredited

Achieved 95% participant satisfaction, CPD accreditation, and measurable learning outcomes across knowledge, confidence, and behaviour change. Participants successfully integrated both domains, applying trauma-informed psychological support alongside technical security protocols in real client scenarios.
