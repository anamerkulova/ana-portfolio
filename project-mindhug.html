Blended Learning Programme Design

Psychology + Cybersecurity CPD Course

Programme Design CPD Accredited Blended Learning 30 Learners

The Challenge

Design and deliver CPD-accredited blended learning programme for 30 coaches with no cybersecurity background, combining psychological principles with technical security concepts in accessible, engaging format.

My Approach

Collaborated with psychology and cybersecurity SMEs to translate complex technical content into relatable scenarios. Designed multimodal learning experience combining presentations, roleplay simulations, peer teaching activities, and performance-based assessment.

Applied instructional design principles (ADDIE, Bloom's Taxonomy) and adult learning theory to create scaffolded progression from foundational concepts to practical application.

Key Design Decisions

  • Translated technical jargon into relatable, real-world scenarios
  • Built scaffolded progression from awareness to application
  • Integrated roleplay simulations for skill practice
  • Designed peer teaching activities to reinforce learning
  • Created performance-based assessments aligned to learning objectives

Impact & Outcomes

30 Coaches Trained
CPD Accredited

Knowledge & Confidence

All participants demonstrated knowledge improvement on post-programme assessments and reported increased confidence in supporting clients through cybersecurity incidents.

Behavior Change & Application

Follow-up conversations revealed tangible changes in how participants approached their work:

  • One participant who previously viewed compliance as "such a chore" became an advocate, eventually speaking publicly about cybersecurity at professional events
  • Several participants (5 of 30) shifted their self-perception from "I'm not a technical person" to "I can communicate in this language"—enabling them to engage more confidently with technical professionals
  • Participants educated peers about cyber threats in their own organizations, translating technical concepts for non-technical colleagues
  • Multiple participants pursued further specialized training in cybersecurity topics, indicating sustained interest beyond the programme

Key Insight

The most significant outcome wasn't just knowledge gain—it was identity shift. Participants who previously avoided technical contexts began seeing themselves as capable of navigating that world, which opened new professional possibilities.
