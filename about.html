About Me

I'm a learning designer who learned from teaching diverse audiences that effective learning requires both solid design and psychological safety.

My journey started with educational psychology—studying how people learn, what motivates behaviour change, and how to create environments where learning thrives. I taught English to diverse adult learners across cultural contexts, earning my DELTA qualification and discovering my passion for designing learning experiences, not just delivering them.

This led me to programme design and facilitation, where I combined instructional design frameworks with my psychology background. I've taken a team through CPD accreditation of our cybersecurity/psychology programme, led trauma-informed co-design with vulnerable communities, and developed learning resources for adult professionals.

What sets my work apart

  • Educational psychology expertise: Evidence-based understanding of how adults learn.
  • Trauma-informed practice: Creating psychologically safe environments where all learners can thrive.
  • Instructional design nerd: Julie Dirksen's book was on my birthday wishlist.
  • Facilitation mastery: Building trust, managing group dynamics, responsive adaptation.
  • AI capabilities: Leveraging generative AI to enhance (not replace) human-centred learning.

I'm particularly interested in

  • Designing learning experiences for diverse adult populations.
  • Creating inclusive, accessible learning that honours different learning needs.
  • Working collaboratively with subject matter experts or faculty.
  • Measuring impact beyond satisfaction scores—real behaviour change and results.
  • Making trauma-informed practice accessible to learning professionals.

Currently, I'm contributing to UX for Change developing trauma-informed learning resources and exploring how AI can enhance learning design, particularly for language learners and tutors.

I'm seeking opportunities to bring instructional design and trauma-informed facilitation to adult learning contexts, such as corporate training, higher education, professional development, or EdTech.
